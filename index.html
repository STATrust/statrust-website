THE PROBLEM

AI is moving faster.
Trust must keep up.

AI agents are no longer static tools. They search, decide, write code, and increasingly improve themselves. But a better benchmark score does not necessarily mean a truly better or statistically valid agent.

STATrust brings statistical thinking into the agent lifecycle: understanding what an agent has learned, what its evaluation actually proves, and when it is safe to move forward.
